# V11

Changes from V10:

- Pet release home records include date, recipient and home-care notes. Admins finalize directly; staff submit for approval.
- Missed-checkup attendance records show that the owner did not attend. They preserve completed-visit totals and the last clinic-visit date. Existing schedules can optionally be rescheduled, with reminders updating after approval.
- Dates, duplicates, future appointments, changed schedules and deceased-pet restrictions are validated. Existing history and treatment status remain intact.

Download versions/V11_VetZone.zip for the current standalone project. Previous releases remain in versions/V9_VetZone.zip and versions/V10_VetZone.zip, with their own branches. The complete current application is at the repository root.
